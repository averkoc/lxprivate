##  Workshop Report Policy: Partnering for Success

This policy is designed to ensure a fair, efficient, and timely feedback process for **all** students. By setting clear standards, we can focus less on administrative corrections and more on your learning.  



---

### 1. 🎯 Submission Standards: Getting It Right the First Time

You get full workshop scores by submitting correct and complete report in your first submisson before deadline. 
> Please note: The provided instructional videos and steps contain all the information required to produce a fully complete and compliant report.

| Status | Required Action | Your Benefit | Maximum Grade Awarded |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Initial Submission** | **Complete** and submitted on time. | Maximum score achieved immediately! | **100%** |
| **Re-submission (1st Attempt)** | Corrected to **Complete** (within 7 days of receiving the 'INC' grade). | The opportunity to pass, despite initial errors. | **80%** (Max) |
| **Re-submission (2nd Attempt)** | Still Incomplete or Non-Compliant. | Time is better spent on the next assignment. | **0% (Final Grade)** |

### 2. ⚙️ Technical Support: Proactive Problem Solving

We want to help you overcome technical hurdles *before* the deadline. This process ensures we can provide timely support when you need it most.

| Rule | Your Action | Purpose (How it Helps You) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Use the Support Box** | Report all problems in the dedicated "Technical Problem Submission Box." | Centralizes issues so the instructor can address them quickly. |
| **2. Report Early** | The support box closes **2 days before the final report deadline.** | Forces an early start, guaranteeing time for you to receive help and still finish. |
| **3. Provide Proof** | Your submission must include a **screenshot of the actual error.** | Allows the instructor to diagnose the problem accurately and quickly. |

> 🔑 **Key Takeaway:** If a technical issue prevents you from submitting a complete report, you **must** use the official support box and submit error proof by its deadline. A simple note or excuse after the fact will unfortunately result in a **0%** grade, as it prevents us from providing timely assistance.

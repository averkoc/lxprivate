Export MP4 does everything for you: it records the visuals, mixes in your original audio file untouched, and encodes a ready-to-upload .mp4 — right in your browser, nothing leaves your computer. The first time you use it in a session it downloads a one-time ~30 MB converter; after that, exports are quick.

Prefer to do it yourself? "Export raw .webm" gives you the silent, frame-synced video only — combine it with your original audio using:

ffmpeg -i score-follower-video-only.webm -i "your-audio-file.wav" -map 0:v -map 1:a -vf "scale=trunc(iw/2)*2:trunc(ih/2)*2" -c:v libx264 -crf 20 -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -b:a 192k -shortest outputfile.mp4
